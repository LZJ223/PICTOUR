工具：内置 imagegen。角色和画风参考分别为游戏中的完整机器人、BookSpitit.png、Tree_03.png 和主菜单动态背景渲染图。

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: original polished promotional key art for an existing indie 2.5D picture-book puzzle platformer.
Output: one landscape image, EXACT 16:9 aspect ratio, preferably 3840 x 2160 pixels; at least 1920 x 1080. Opaque full-bleed illustration, no border.

Reference roles: Image 1 is the canonical assembled player robot character reference. Image 2 is the canonical floating Book Spirit character reference. Image 3 is the canonical geometric tree design and halftone texture reference. Image 4 is an in-game forest screenshot ONLY for the established palette, environment language and overall identity. These are REFERENCES, NOT edit targets: create a newly drawn cohesive promotional composition, not a screenshot, not a literal paste-up of existing sprites, not a character sheet.

Primary request: make the game's depth-layer switching and 2.5D optical-illusion puzzles visually legible through the environment itself. A lush, whimsical forest picture-book world unfolds into three or four distinct, offset paper scenery planes. Show the thin cream paper edges, visible gaps and overlap between those planes. A broken grassy pathway on one depth plane aligns with a separate ledge farther back, seeming to form one impossible continuous bridge from the viewer's viewpoint. A graceful curled paper strip and slightly offset registration contours suggest one forest layer sliding forward while another recedes, WITHOUT diagram arrows or UI. The depth-layer alignment is the main visual idea, not just generic platforms or floating islands.

Composition: a single dynamic panoramic illustration with a clear focal group: the robot on the lip of a grassy paper ledge, poised to pass into the next depth layer, and the small floating Book Spirit guiding it. The world grows upward from the subtly visible curved pages of an open picture book along the lower edge, filling the frame with one integrated forest. Slightly oblique, near-ground eye-level stage perspective, not an overhead tabletop photograph. Fan the scenery planes apart enough to reveal the depth illusion, retaining a readable continuous adventure scene. Frame the characters with geometric trees and a few small storybook houses; preserve a clear silhouette and visual breathing space. The hero should be large enough to recognize, about one quarter of image height, rather than a tiny screenshot-sized figure. The paper mechanism and impossible aligned ledges must remain easy to read.

Character invariants, extremely important:
- Preserve Image 1's distinctive muted blue-gray robot, proportions and construction: a rounded-rectangular floating head with a small gap above the boxy torso, a simple short horizontal propeller bar on top, two narrow white oval LED eyes on its lighter front face, and a single compact continuous caterpillar-track base with THREE visible wheels. Rounded geometric panels, no humanoid legs, no added arms or hands, no antenna redesign, no big circular binocular eyes, no mouth. Express life through the pose and angle without redesigning it.
- Preserve Image 2's exact Book Spirit identity: small hovering brown/reddish closed book, cream layered pages, two large expressive white-and-black oval eyes near the spine/page edge, slightly crooked book silhouette, bold black hand-drawn contour and thick white sticker-like outer outline. No human body, limbs, wings, hat or new face design.
- Preserve Image 3's tree language: clearly geometric rounded-rectangle green canopy blocks, brown branching trunks with graphic right-angle bends, black ink contours and selected polka-dot halftone areas. Do not replace these with generic realistic trees.

Style/medium: hand-illustrated cartoon picture-book key art with an intentional collage/paper-cut graphic vocabulary, all freshly redrawn as one composition. Black pen contours with subtle natural wobble, flat color fills, TWO-TONE CEL SHADOWS to describe volume, crisp simple geometric silhouettes. Use tasteful halftone polka dots on some canopy and paper shadow areas, and white cutout outlines around the focal characters and a few foreground shapes. Only very subtle paper grain; bright and clean, not dirty distressed cardboard. Match the existing game's cyan sky, vivid leafy greens, warm cream paper, brown trunks/roofs and muted blue robot. Distant scenery can be softer and paler, but keep the focal illustration crisp. Friendly, curious, adventurous mood with strong thumbnail readability.

Text: NONE. Absolutely no descriptive text, title, logo, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, watermarks, UI, buttons, speech bubbles or instructional arrows anywhere. All book pages and covers must contain only pictorial/abstract elements, no writing.

Avoid: simple asset collage, copied screenshot layout, photorealism, rendered plastic/clay/3D-toy look, smooth airbrushed volume gradients, anime redesign, generic robot redesign, photorealistic foliage, cluttered infographic, excessive magical glow, a random pile of floating islands without readable depth planes.
