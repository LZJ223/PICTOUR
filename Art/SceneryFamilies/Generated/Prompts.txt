Oldwood_Family
Use case: stylized-concept. Production 2D orthographic SIDE VIEW asset family atlas for a handprinted fairy-tale platform puzzle game. Reference garden is STYLE ONLY. Match its dry woodcut ink, rough missing-ink patches, muted aubergine/dusty rose with cream scuffing, calm strange geometry. Genuinely transparent background, no paper rectangle. Square canvas with a STRICT TWO BY TWO equal cell grid, four isolated full objects with generous transparent margins between cells. Each object individually fully visible. No labels, characters, numbers, shadows or scenery background. Asset silhouettes must remain beautiful and readable at small scale. Each object is a UNIQUE STRUCTURAL DERIVATIVE of one parent material, not duplicates recolored or resized. Flat front/side 2D silhouettes without perspective top faces. Irregular broken contours naturally include roughly horizontal broad 50-80unit resting shoulders, gentle transitions, no regular steps, no manufactured ladder or platform planks.
Family: ancient dark plum heartwood with pale dry ring lines, split grain, tiny dusty rose leaf impressions and a few worn bark areas. Top-left: a broad low split root seat/stump, 160wide100tall, asymmetric split top creates one large flat natural shoulder, tapering gnarled roots. Top-right: a long LOW horizontal fallen root bridge, 400wide130tall, left thick broken shoulder, middle smooth worn hump, right root sweeps down into a broad flat end; three separate wide worn landing regions with uneven height differences ~40units, broad hollows beneath. Bottom-left: a leaning broken trunk 230wide240tall with two broad naturally broken branch forks at different heights and a rounded broken top; no fan canopy; do not make ladder steps. Bottom-right: an ancient split hollow stump arch 300wide240tall, large transparent hollow reaches bottom; broken old roots form asymmetric supporting feet, one branch stub sticks sideways as a broad landing. All four visibly share wood material and motifs but shapes genuinely differ. Original storybook print, dreamlike yet practical physical contours.
